How to Play

Everything you need to fly, score, and win.

Getting Started

1

Create Your Account

Register with your username, email, and phone number. Your username is what appears on the leaderboard, so choose wisely! You can also sign in with Google for a faster start.

2

Tap to Flap

Your bird falls due to gravity. Tap the screen (or press Space) to flap and rise. Time your flaps carefully — too fast and you hit the top, too slow and you crash to the ground. The sweet spot is a steady rhythm.

3

Dodge the Pipes

Green pipes come from the right at increasing intervals. Fly your bird through the gap between the top and bottom pipe. Touching any pipe or the ground ends your game immediately. Each pair of pipes you pass scores you +1 point.

4

Beat Your Best Score

Your Best Score is saved forever — it never resets. Your Season Score resets at the end of each month. Play multiple games to climb both boards. Only your highest score per game counts.

5

Compete Every Month

The leaderboard resets at the end of every month. The top 3 players each season are saved to the Hall of Fame forever and may qualify for a monthly reward. A new season then begins — fresh start for everyone!

💡 Pro Tips

🎯Don't spam tap — one controlled flap at a time keeps the bird stable.
👁️Watch the gap, not the bird. Your eyes should be on where you're going.
😮‍💨Breathe. Panic causes overcorrection. Stay calm past pipe 10 and you're flying.
🔁Play consistently every day in a season — your best game is what counts.
🔇Mute distractions. The score sound helps you keep rhythm — use it.
📱On mobile, tap the centre of the screen for the most reliable input.
